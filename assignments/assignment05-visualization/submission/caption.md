**Final Figure Caption**
This figure shows mean BMI by income group across NHANES survey cycles among adults aged 20–80 using the prepared NHANES Health Equity classroom dataset. The analysis is descriptive and unweighted. Records with missing BMI, income group, cycle, or age values were excluded, along with income-cycle groups containing fewer than 30 observations. The figure supports descriptive comparisons, not causal conclusions or population level based estimates.
