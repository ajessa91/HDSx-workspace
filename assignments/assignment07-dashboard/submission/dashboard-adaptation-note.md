# Dashboard Adaptation Note

## Audience

The intended audience for this asssignment is a health anthropologist studying socioeconomic inequalities and how that realtes to health patterns and/or health outcomes among the adult populaiton, specifcally looking at low income groups. 

## Dashboard Question

How has average BMI levels changed over time within adults in the low income group?  What insights might be uncovered in regards to socioecomic health inequalites? 

## Control And Visualization

For this dashboard I selected low income groups to explore how average BMI has changed over time among adults with fewer finical resources compared to those in other groups. From a health anthropology lens this group is rather relevant because socioeconomic status can shape what access people may or may not receive. For example, acess to healthy food, healthcare, and other resources that influence health. Examining these patterns may help raise questions about broader social and structural inequalities, while the dashboard cannot determine their causes it can give us a visual depiction of the data. 

- Control or interaction: The selected income group which is Low Income (<1.3)  can be changed by editing selected_income and re rendering the dashboard.
- Visualization affected: A line graph showing mean BMI across nhanes survey cycles from 1999–2000 to 2021–2023.
- What changes when the control changes: We will she changes in the graph, title, summary table to show the mean BMI values for the new selected income group which for this dashbaord is low income groups.

## Limitation
This dashboard cannot establish whether income causes changes in BMI or represent national population trends. This is because the analysis uses unweighted descriptive data, the findings cannot be generalized to the national population.
